The Global and Daily Math of Destruction

We sort plastics and feel guilt over the heating. In the Czech Republic, an average household emits roughly 59 kg of CO₂ per day. But the math collapses when the world goes to war.

The Global Military Complex accounts for 5.5% of all greenhouse gas emissions — 7.53 million tonnes of CO₂ every single day. If it were a country, it would be the 4th-largest polluter on Earth.

"The military's environmental exceptionalism allows them to pollute with impunity…" — Dr. Benjamin Neimark

Conflict Spikes — Split by Sides

  • Gaza Conflict: ~4,500 t CO₂/day (Israel >99%, Hamas <1%)
  • Ukraine War: ~213,000 t CO₂/day (Russia ~70%, Ukraine ~30%)
  • 1991 Gulf War oil wells: 1.5–2 million t CO₂/day

To offset one day of Ukraine emissions you would need the entire population of Prague to live carbon-free for ~15 years.

The Micro-Math: Cost of a Single Strike

  • One Hand Grenade: 5 kg CO₂
  • One 500 lb Bomb: hundreds of kg manufacture + 125 kg detonation
  • One Cruise Missile: 30,000+ kg CO₂
  • One Fighter Jet (1 h mission): 14–15 t CO₂
  • One Drone Attack on Factory: tens of thousands t toxic gases

The Carbon Debt of Rebuilding

27% of Ukraine war footprint is post-war reconstruction (cement & steel). Direct warfare still ~155,000 t CO₂/day.

The Invisible Legacy: 300 Years or "Eat It All"

  • Kakhovka Dam toxic flush (landmines, sewage, heavy metals, nanoplastics)
  • Heavy metals in soil (lead, cadmium, mercury — 100–300 years to clean)
  • Pulverized nanoplastics & asbestos dust

"Dear future generations: Please accept our apologies. We were rolling drunk on petroleum." — Kurt Vonnegut

The Quiet Rebellion

A single artillery shell erases your year of effort. Yet sorting your bin is quiet rebellion — proof we still choose to build and preserve.
